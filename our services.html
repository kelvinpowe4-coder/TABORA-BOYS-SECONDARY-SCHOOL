Our Services

Supporting academic excellence, discipline, leadership and holistic student development.

What We Offer

Tabora Boys Secondary School provides an environment designed to support students in academic development, personal discipline, leadership and co-curricular development.

📚

Academic Education

The school provides secondary education with emphasis on academic achievement, critical thinking and preparation for further education.

🎓

Advanced Level Studies

Students are provided with opportunities to pursue advanced secondary education in different subject combinations, including science and arts-related combinations, include PCM, PCB, HGL $ PMC

🔬

Science Education

Science-oriented studies support students in developing analytical thinking, problem-solving skills and preparation for science-related careers.

⚽

Sports and Games

Students participate in sports and games as part of school life, helping to promote physical fitness, teamwork, discipline and healthy competition.

📝

Academic Preparation

Students take part in scheduled academic preparation sessions that support revision, independent study and examination preparation.

🛡️

Discipline and Character Development

The school promotes discipline, responsibility, good conduct and values that prepare students for responsible citizenship.

👨‍🎓

Leadership Development

Students are encouraged to develop leadership, responsibility, confidence and the ability to contribute positively to society.

🤝

Moral and Religious Development

School activities include opportunities that support moral development, respect, responsibility and spiritual growth.

Student Life

Student life at Tabora Boys combines classroom learning with preparation sessions, sports, personal discipline and other activities that contribute to the development of well-rounded students.

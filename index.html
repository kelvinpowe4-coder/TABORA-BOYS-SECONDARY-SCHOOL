Welcome to Tabora Boys Secondary School. We are committed to providing quality education and developing responsible and successful students.

Tabora Boys Secondary School is one of Tanzania’s historic and renowned secondary schools, with a long-standing tradition of academic excellence and leadership. The school has played an important role in Tanzania’s educational history and has educated many distinguished Tanzanians, including Mwalimu Julius Kambarage Nyerere, the Father of the Nation and Tanzania’s first President. With its rich heritage, Tabora Boys continues to uphold the values of education, discipline, leadership, and service to the nation.
